# Seasonal selling calendar

This reference lists the dates and preparation windows used by `shopify-operations-brief` to add a timely seasonal reminder to the “What to do next” section.

---

## When to start preparing

Seasonal campaigns often need product images, stock, offers, customer emails, landing pages, and product bundles ready a few weeks early.

| Holiday / Event | Target Date | Preparation Window | Key Campaign Advisory |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Year / Fresh Start** | Jan 01 | Dec 15 - Dec 31 | New Year resolution bundles, inventory clearance, fresh fitness/lifestyle messaging. |
| **Valentine's Day** | Feb 14 | Jan 15 - Feb 05 | Gift guides, couples bundles, express shipping deadlines, gift cards. |
| **Mother's Day (UK/US)** | Mar / May | 3-4 weeks prior | Personalized gifts, higher-value gift bundles, Mother's Day promo codes, early bird discounts. |
| **Father's Day** | Mid June | May 15 - Jun 05 | Tech/outdoor/lifestyle bundles, dad gift collections, tiered discounts. |
| **Summer Sale & Prime Day** | July | Jun 15 - Jul 10 | Mid-year clearance, summer travel/outdoor bundles, flash sales. |
| **Back to School / Labor Day** | Late Aug - Early Sep | Aug 01 - Aug 25 | Student/family promotions, volume discounts (Buy 2 Get 1), bulk bundle deals. |
| **Halloween** | Oct 31 | Sep 15 - Oct 10 | Themed collections, limited-time spooky bundles, early October shipping cutoff notices. |
| **Black Friday & Cyber Monday (BFCM)** | Late Nov | Oct 01 - Nov 10 | **The biggest peak of the year**: Tiered cart thresholds (Spend $100 Get $20), VIP early access email lists, stock buildup, site speed & checkout testing. |
| **Christmas & Holiday Season** | Dec 25 | Nov 01 - Dec 10 | Holiday gift packaging, guaranteed delivery countdown timers, last-minute digital gift cards, post-Christmas clearance prep. |

---

## When a reminder appears

The report checks today's date in the shop's timezone. It only adds a “Prepare ahead” reminder when an event is close enough; it does not add a generic task every month.

- Back to School / Labor Day: 31 days before September 1.
- Halloween: 45 days before October 31.
- BFCM: 45 days before the fourth Thursday in November.
- Christmas: 45 days before December 25.

The displayed countdown is calculated from the actual current date. It is advisory only; it does not create a discount, edit a campaign, or reserve inventory.
